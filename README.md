# 🧺 Washify - Wash. Press. Done.

**Your Laundry, Our Responsibility**

Washify is a modern doorstep laundry and ironing service designed specifically for students and busy professionals. We solve the everyday hassle of laundry with convenient pickup, professional cleaning, and timely delivery.

---

## 🌟 Features

### For Customers
- **Doorstep Pickup & Delivery** - No need to visit laundry shops
- **Professional Washing** - Fabric-specific care and stain removal
- **Expert Ironing** - Crisp, ready-to-wear clothes
- **Quick Turnaround** - 24-48 hour delivery
- **Real-time Tracking** - Know exactly where your laundry is
- **Flexible Scheduling** - Book pickups at your convenience
- **Student Discounts** - Special pricing for hostel and PG residents
- **Quality Assurance** - Satisfaction guaranteed

### Services Offered
1. **Wash & Fold** - Starting ₹49/kg
2. **Wash & Iron** - Starting ₹69/kg (Most Popular)
3. **Dry Cleaning** - Starting ₹99/piece
4. **Express Service** - Same-day delivery available
